⚡ Lightning Strike

A mech auto-battler · you build them, they fight themselves

You never pilot your mechs. You build up to three and set their instincts in the garage — then assign them to battlefield lanes and watch each fight resolve on its own.

1 · Build mechs (6 parts each)

  • Head, Core, two Arms, Backpack, Legs. Each part adds stats and often an ability; weapons always grant an attack.
  • Every part has its own HP. Your Core is that mech's life — lose it and the mech dies. A downed weapon drops to a weak "broken" attack.
  • Legs set a mobility tag (ground / tread / hover / swim); a jetpack adds fly, a jump-jet adds jump.

2 · Deploy across lanes (terrain gates)

  • Each mission has 1–3 lanes, each with a terrain. A mech can only be assigned to a lane its tags allow — an air lane needs FLY, water needs SWIM, highland bars treads/hover.
  • The right lane needs the right chassis. If nothing fits, rebuild in the garage.

3 · Hold assist points · cross chasms

  • At an assist point a mech can hold to lend a shot into an adjacent lane's next fight (great for support builds with "when an ally is assisting" tactics), or push on.
  • A chasm segment is free for JUMP/FLY mechs; anyone else takes attrition crossing it.

4 · Between fights — spend Lightning

  • Damage carries over per lane. Bank ⚡ Lightning (shared) from wins: Repair HP, Full-Repair a disabled part, or Pre-load an attack. Clear every lane to win.

Early prototype — combat + lane systems only, no story/economy yet. Feedback very welcome.
