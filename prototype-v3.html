⚡ Lightning Strike

A mech auto-battler · you build it, it fights itself

You never pilot your mech in a fight. You build it and set its instincts in the garage — then watch the battle resolve on its own. Your cleverness is decided before the shooting starts.

1 · Build a mech (6 parts)

  • Head, Core, two Arms, Backpack, Legs. Each part adds stats and often an ability; weapons always grant an attack.
  • Every part has its own HP. Your Core is your life — lose it and the mech dies.
  • Knock out any other part and its ability goes dark. A downed weapon drops to a weak "broken" attack — never fully toothless.

2 · Set its instincts (tactics)

  • Each ability gets a firing rule — e.g. "if I'm hurt", "target the weakest enemy".
  • Attacks also pick a part to aim at: the enemy's Core (go for the kill) or a subsystem like their Weapon (disable it first). Once a targeted part is down, your fire shifts to the Core automatically.

3 · Watch the fight (or skip it)

  • Combat plays out in rounds, hands-off. Watch it, or hit Skip. It's deterministic — same setup, same result.
  • Enemies are either mechs/bosses with targetable parts, or monolithic tanks/swarms with a single health pool.

4 · Between fights — spend Lightning

  • Damage and knocked-out parts carry over down the lane. Bank ⚡ Lightning from winning, then spend it: Repair HP, Full-Repair a disabled part (the only way to revive one), or Pre-load an attack.

Try this

  • CROSSFIRE — the default aim loses. Re-aim your big gun at the sniper: kill its Core, or disable its Weapon.
  • THE WARDEN boss can't be out-slugged — disable its Doom Array nuke or get deleted.
