⚡ SPRITE SANDBOX

Drop in any candidate art — a single pose or a frame strip — to see it on the 64×64 frame with anchor guides, recolored across all 5 schemes + the enemy ramp, and linted against the master palette (so you know whether it'll recolor). Nothing is saved; this is just a viewer. To make art live in the game, see the note below.

⬇ Drop image(s) here, or click to pick — PNGs from Aseprite/Midjourney/anywhere
Where to put art so it renders in the game:
• Quick look / compare styles → here (drag-drop, no files to place).
• Replace a real part → save your PNG to assets/mechs/<slot>/<id>.png (filename = part id, e.g. assets/mechs/rarm/plasma.png; horizontal strip of 64×64 frames, or one 64×64 image), then run python tools/bundle_sprites.py and refresh render-demo.html.
• ⚠ Don't re-run gen_placeholders.py after repainting — it overwrites art. Use bundle_sprites.py.
• Paint on the master palette (assets/palette.gpl) for the recolor to work — this sandbox's lint tells you if you did.
